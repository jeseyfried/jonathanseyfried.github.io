## Today’s Document 
- Subject: Joan's health has suffered another 'accident'
- Date: May 27, 1387
- Day of the Week: Monday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R2053 f11r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12778718)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipients: Ambassadors in Avignon

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: Violant has long had a tendancy to present the health news about Joan in the most optimistic manner, so this document's pessimism stands out; at this moment, Violant might be seriously contemplating what she will do if Joan dies; the recipients 

- Contextualization: the mention of the new moon brings up the close connection between astrology and medical practice in the Middle Ages

- Corroboration: at this point, a large number documents in the podcast should be considered alongside this one as corroboration, so browsing through the [episodes tagged with illness](https://jonathanseyfried.net/historians-notebook/tags/?tag=illness) will allow for a good comparison; this appears to be the first time that Violant mentions that Joan has had difficulty sleeping due to his condition

- Close-Reading: until now, I have been translation 'accidents' as misfortune, but perhaps it is better to translate it as something more active, such as 'attacks of sickness'

## What is this document doing?
- This document shows an intent to present an unvarnished and truthful picture about the king's health.
- The document entrusts the recipients with highly sensitive information.

## Questions
- Had Violant become pessimistic about Joan's recovery?
- Why were the ambassadors in Avignon the ones Violant chose to send this sensitive information?
- Did Joan actually have days in May when he seemed fully recovered?
- What kinds of emotional support did Violant have during this anxious period?
- Should this letter be considered evidence that Joan had not really been involved in a lot of the documents issued in his name during the month of May?
