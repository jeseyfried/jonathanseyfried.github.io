## Today’s Document 
- Subject: financing the state in an interfaith context
- Date: May 24, 1387
- Day of the Week: Friday
- Language: Aragonese/Castilian
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R2053 f11r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12778718)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipients: officials of the Jewish and Muslim aljamas of Teruel

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: Violant had a direct governing authority over the aljamas and perhaps this made her more confident that tax increases would be met with compliance; both the Muslim and Jewish officials received the same document, indicating the high level of parity in the treatment at least on the topic of taxes

- Contextualization: medieval Iberian queen lieutenants had, for over a century, held their own portfolios of aljamas and had separate treasury officials; at this time, [Teruel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teruel#History) had for centuries been firmly within the Crown of Aragon as a border city with Valencia and Castile; a recent article article by Asunción Blasco Martínez on the Najarí family provides excellent context on the aljama of Teruel[^abm]

[^abm]:Asunción Blasco Martínez, “Los Najarí, judíos de Teruel: del esplendor al ocaso (siglos XIII-XV),” *Miscelánea de Estudios Árabes y Hebraicos.* Sección Hebreo 74 (November 2025): 73–125.

- Corroboration: the document examined in [Episode 29](e29-jan22) also informas localities of additional taxation and it is worth comparing that one, as well as the more elaborate [May 20th](e147-may20) to today's document in order to get a better idea of when Violant had to make an extra effort to justify new levies, how religious contexts affected her choices, and what language she used to do that; many other documents about the introduction of new taxes can be found by browsing the episodes [tagged with finances](https://jonathanseyfried.net/historians-notebook/tags/?tag=finances); most of the documents tagged with [Jewish History](https://jonathanseyfried.net/historians-notebook/tags/?tag=Jewish+History) and [Muslim History](https://jonathanseyfried.net/historians-notebook/tags/?tag=Muslim+History) also demonstrate the direct governing authority that the monarchs had over the aljamas throughout the Crown of Aragon

- Close-Reading: rather than relying on an invocation of their duty as subjects, Violant uses the phrase 'nos ayud' meaning aid us or help us, and possibly this is nothing more than a typical expression for this kind of tax levy

## What is this document doing?
- This document invokes general loyalty as its justification for the additional burden it is placing on the recipients.
- The document presumes a special relationship between the queen and the community of the aljama.

## Questions
- Why was Teruel chosen for additional taxation at this time and why is this document entered in this register?
- How great of a financial emergency had necessitated these tax levies?
- How would the treasury official named in this document, Johann Ximenez ,have been treated by the leaders of the aljamas?
- How did the different sections of the royal bureaucracy come together to produce this document, as represented in the probata?
