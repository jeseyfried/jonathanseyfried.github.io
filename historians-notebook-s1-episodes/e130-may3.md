## Today’s Document 
- Subject: the evacuation and/or recall of the subjects of the Crown of Aragon currently in Pisa
- Date: May 3, 1387
- Day of the Week: Friday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1826 f124v-125r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12751698)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Joan
- Recipients: the Catalan merchant community in Pisa

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: some kind of political crisis has led to the likely expulsion of Catalan merchants from Pisa; the court officials, if not Joan himself, consider Berenguer Morey a good choice for the diplomatic mission to reconcile with the Pisans; records of Morey as a very wealthy Barcelona merchant exist in several archivals sources

- Contextualization: the dispute between the Crown of Aragon and Pisa was in some ways a consequence of the Great Western Schism, with prominent Pisans throwing their support behind the Pope in Rome as Joan declared support for the Pope in Avignon; at the same time, the conflict appears to have started in 1386, well before Joan's reign, when Pere the Ceremonious kept the Crown of Aragon neutral in the schism; eventually the Catalan merchant community in Pisa bounced back and the modern scholar José Bordes García quotes a 1397 document from a [Datini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_Datini) agent which said the Catalan merchants in Pisa were 'tutti richi'[jbg] 

[^jbg]:José Bordes García, “L’activitat Dels Mercaders Catalans a La Pisa Medieval (Final Del Segle Xiv-Primera Meitat Del Xv),” *Estudis Històrics i Documents Dels Arxius de Protocols*, no. 23 (2015): 75–90, at 80.

- Corroboration: no other documents thus far in the podcast have mentioned the expulsion of Catalan merchants from a foreign city; the safe-passage document for a merchant examined in [Episode 58](e58-feb20) gives us an idea of how it might be difficult for merchants to travel through various localities and the utility of Catalan residents in various ports; Morey is in some ways like Luchino Scarampi, as discussed in [Episode 125](e125-apr28) in that he is a wealthy merchant who becomes involved in resolving a dispute between powerful states

- Close-Reading: the Latin makes close reading difficult for this document, but I would look for indications of how urgent the problem is for the Catalan community in Pisa at the moment

## What is this document doing?
- This document, in issuing advice for evacuation, severely curtails the sense of security for the Catalan merchants in Pisa.
- The document connects a wealthy merchant to diplomatic activity.

## Questions
- What had precipitated the recent escalation of conflict between Pisa and the Catalan merchants?
- How had Berenguer Morey been chosen for this diplomatic mission?
- What role did the Great Western Schism play in the minds of the people involved in the various aspects of this conflict?
