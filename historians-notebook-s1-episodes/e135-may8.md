## Today’s Document 
- Subject: prisoner transport from Perpignan to Barcelona
- Date: May 8, 1387
- Day of the Week: Wednesday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1826 f131v-132r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12751698)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Joan
- Recipients: Officials of Roussillon, Cerdagne, and Perpignan

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: possibly concerned that the authorities in Perpignan might treat Bartolomeo Perich too lightly, Joan uses his authority as head of state to ensure that a pirate faces justice in Barcelona

- Contextualization: pirates were a constant presence in the Medieval Mediterranean; for context about piracy on the Mediterranean, a great place to start is Fernand Braudel's *The Mediterranean and the Mediterranean World in the Age of Philip II*[^fb]

[^fb]:Fernand Braudel, *The Mediterranean and the Mediterranean World in the Age of Philip II*. Volume 1, [print. of the ed. of 1972] (New York: Harper & Row, 1990), 117, 121, 129-131, 134, 138, 151, 155, 234, 244, 278, 291-292, 315, 467, 566, 601, 610, 629, 635, and 641.

- Corroboration: in [Episode 116](e116-apr19) we examined a document about piracy in which Joan considers them a threat and then in [Episode 125](e125-apr28) we looked at a document about a pirate clearly allied to Catalan interests

- Close-Reading: this document employs the word 'maleficis' in a way that seems to have nothing to do with magic or evil sorcery 

## What is this document doing?
- This document portrays piracy wholly as a criminal category.
- The document reveals the importance of different jurdisdictions and forums in outcomes of criminal cases. 

## Questions
- How much involvement did Joan have in the authorship of this document?
- Who was Bartolomeo Perich and what kinds of acts of piracy had he committed?
- Was the rivalry with Genoa involved in this case?
