## Today’s Documents 
- Subject: initiating an intercession of holy prayer for Joan's health
- Date: April 29, 1387
- Day of the Week: Monday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number for Violant's letter: ACA CR R2053 f7r-v
- [Link to PARES for Violant's Letter](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12778718)
- Archival Reference Number for Joan's letter: ACA CR R1751 f53r-v 
- [Link to PARES for Joan's Letter](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/4801766)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant (Joan's register has Violant's scribe in the probata)
- Recipients: Violant's letters to James the Cardinal of Valencia and to several monasteries; Joan's summons to Abrahim, a Muslim doctor in Xàtiva
