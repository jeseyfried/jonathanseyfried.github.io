## Today’s Document 
- Subject: Recommendation for Arnaldo Torrelles
- Date: February 2, 1387
- Day of the Week: Saturday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1923 f19v-20r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12777646)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Joan
- Recipients: Bishop of Tortosa

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: another example of the top secular authority of a realm, in this case the King of the Crown of Aragon, interceding in the process of filling an ecclesiastical position; as king, Joan's recommendation will likely persuade the Bishop of Tortosa to give Arnaldo Torrelles the posting

- Contextualization: the intertwined character of royal and ecclesiastical authority within the Crown of Aragon; the situtation of Tortosa as a key municipality firmly within the sovereignty of the Crown of Aragon

- Corroboration: with several letters now examined in which the King and Queen of the Crown of Aragon intercede in the process of ecclesiastical appointments, this document appears to fit in a well-established pattern of shared authority between the state and the church on the topic of who will occupy positions of power in municipalities across the realm

- Close-Reading: the inclusion of Raymundo Barbera and the mention of a fine of 1000 florins in connection to his actions raises questions about the meaning and purpose of the entire document

## What is this document doing?
- This document reinforces the connection between secular and religious authority in the Crown of Aragon.
- The document also activates the social network of Arnaldo Torrelles to secure a post.

## Questions
- Is this a straightforward recommendation letter or does the additional text hint at larger problems in Tortosa?
- What is Arnaldo Torrelles's background?
- What kind of position was Arnaldo being considered for by the Bishop of Tortosa?
- Who was Raymundo Barbera? 
- Is the threatened fine a warning to Barbera? What behavior or action is the fine referring to?
- Was the governance, secular and ecclesiastical, of Tortosa stable or chaotic at this time?

## Additional Notes
Sicily became a territory of the Crown of Aragon in the context of the [War of the Sicilian Vespers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Sicilian_Vespers) in the late thirteenth century. The role of Sicily in the disagreement between Pere the Ceremonious and Joan about Joan's second marriage was discussed in [Episode 7](e7-dec31).
