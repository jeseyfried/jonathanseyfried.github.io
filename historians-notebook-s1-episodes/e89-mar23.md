## Today’s Document 
- Subject: arrangements for a pension for Thomas Solers, based on earlier documents
- Date: March 23, 1387
- Day of the Week: Saturday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1822 f142v
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/4801971)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipients: Thomas Solers and other officials

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: the existence of the earlier document quoted inside this one indicates that the scribes had the ability to refer back to archival records from years prior

- Contextualization: the difficulty of the paleography in this document presents a contextualization challenge, as I am not even certain of the location of the people mentioned in it; however, we do see at work here the legitimacy of Iberian queen lieutenancy as Violant now weighs in on a matter that had previously been addressed by the king, Joan's father

- Corroboration: while my difficulty understanding this document makes corroboration challenging, it might be helpful to compare this document to others that mention hefty penalties, such as the documents examined in [Episode 34](e34-jan27), [Episode 40](e40-feb2), and [Episode 84](e84-mar18)

- Close-Reading: Violant chose to issue a threat of a penalty of one hundred florins and that lends urgency to the resolution of whatever problem that has to do with Thomas Solers

## What is this document doing?
- This document weaves together the bureaucratic processes under different members of the royal family across a span of at least two years. 
- The document presumes that the queen's status as arbiter is as legitimate as the past king's.

## Questions
- How did an earlier ruling by Joan's father wind its way through the system to become part of Violant's portfolio?
- What was the initial complaint and why was the earlier ruling not sufficient to address it?
- What aspect of this situation merited the hefty penalty of one hundred florins?
- What were the offices or social status of all the people mentioned in this document?
