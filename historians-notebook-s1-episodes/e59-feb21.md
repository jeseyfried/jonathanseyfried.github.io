## Today’s Document 
- Subject: Christians taking property from Jews in Alcañiz
- Date: February 21, 1387
- Day of the Week: Thursday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1822 f132r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/4801971)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipients: royal officials of Alcañiz

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: Violant's purpose in this document was to bring the authority of the queen to improve the defenses of the Jewish community of Alcañiz against marauding Christians; the audience, royal authorities in Alcañiz, would likely consider the consequences of defying the queen's authority, but Benajamin Gampel has demonstrated that many municpal leaders were adept at obfuscating and dodging directives from the monarchs[^brg]

[^brg]:Benjamin R. Gampel, *Anti-Jewish Riots in the Crown of Aragon and the Royal Response, 1391-1392* (Cambridge University Press, 2016)

- Contextualization: the centuries-long context for the Iberian peninsula is one of interfaith encounter that could at times look like multicultural tolerance but often involved competition and conflict; the first documented presence of Jews in Alcañiz dates from 1280 and by 1370 records indicate about sixty Jewish families in the town, what was probably about 600 people[^clc]

[^clc]:Carlos Laliena Corbera, “Orígenes y extinción de una aljama judaica: Alcañiz, 1280-1414,” *Destierros Aragoneses : Ponencias y Comunicaciones*, 1988, 115–126, at 115 and 120.

- Corroboration: this document corroborates Benjamin Gampel's interpretation of Violant's orientation toward elite Jews and Jewish communities in the Crown of Aragon; other episodes have helped to outine aspects of interfaith encounter in the Crown of Aragon examined in [Episode 19](e19-jan12), [Episode 29](e29-jan22), [Episode 30](e30-jan23), and [Episode 49](e49-feb11)

- Close-Reading: the identification of the recipients of this letter is not the usual 'locumtensi' but possibly closely related to that concept

## What is this document doing?
- This document attempts to leverage the power of the queen to change the dynamics of interfaith conflict in a faraway town.
- The document refutes Christian concerns about the economic relationship they had with Jews in Alcañiz.
