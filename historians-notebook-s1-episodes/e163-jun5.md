## Today’s Document 
- Subject: Joan's attempt to solve a longstanding battle over who rules the Els Ports region
- Date: June 5, 1387
- Day of the Week: Wednesday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1826 f150v-151v
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12751698)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Joan
- Recipients: all of the officials and magistrates around the town of Morella

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: Joan refers to an earlier effort by Violant to impose a settlement on the fighting factions, and it appears that this region had also been in the Queen's portfolio during the reign of Sibilla; in this document, Joan uses his authority as king to attempt to compel the fighting parties in Els Ports to end their feuding 

- Contextualization: there had been decades of fighting between local lords and councils over which authority would have the dominant political role in the Els Ports region[^vrp]; it is clear, though, that the current document failed to solve the problem since the dispute over provincial rule in Morella became a large part of the Cortes of Monzón[^jsa]; the Cortes of Monzón met in 1388-1389 and Joan walked away in frustration leaving the negotiation in the hands of Violant, who handled the finalization of the Cortes, a resolution that kept the royal court from getting its funds cut off[^tb]

[^vrp]:Vicent Royo Pérez, “Árbitros y mediadores en el mundo rural valenciano durante la Baja Edad Media: Els Ports y El Maestrat (s. XIII-XIV),” *Espacio Tiempo y Forma. Serie III, Historia Medieval*, no. 32 (April 2019): 379–412. 

[^jsa]:José Sánchez Adell, “La comunidad de Morella y sus aldeas durante la Baja Edad Media: notas y documentos,” *Estudis Castellonencs*, no. 1 (1983): 73–184.

[^tb]:Thomas Bisson, *Medieval Crown of Aragon: A Short History* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1986), 123.

- Corroboration: the document examined in [Episode 160](e160-jun2) is also about Morella, but I could not find a connection between that and today's document other than a general sense of instability and violence in the region; 

- Close-Reading: in this document Violant is referred to as 'illustres Yolant regina aragonis consoris nostra carissima' which is basically Latin for 'molt cara companyona'; the words for 'destruction' and 'ruin' toward the end of this document reflect the high level of ongoing violence

## What is this document doing?
- This document transfers the problem-solving responsibility in this region from the queen to the king.
- The document presumes ultimate royal authority over the Els Ports region. 

## Questions
- Was this document essentially an escalation from Violant's authority to Joan's authority?
- To what extent, if any, did the feuding leaders in Els Ports respect royal authority?
- Was Violant, or members of her court, involved in the creation of this document?
- Who were the major leaders in the factional fighting in Els Ports?
- Was the murder investigation from the June 2nd document related to this larger picture of factional violence?
