## Today’s Document 
- Subject: Joan commands that the heiress Bernardona Vines be brought to the Governor of Valencia
- Date: April 3, 1387
- Day of the Week: Wednesday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1825 f97v
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12751618)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Joan
- Recipients: Francesc Vines and Garcia de Loris

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: as king, Joan had the final say in any civil disputes as well as matters of state and criminal cases; the two men in this letter perhaps had a desire to appropriate some of Bernardona's inheritance

- Contextualization: the monarchs of the Middle Ages would sometimes rule on cases that in modern political systems would never rise to the level of the head of state; there were levels of jurisdictions for courts and local magistrates, but the monarch still held the position of final arbiter for difficult cases and hear appeals

- Corroboration: this is the first custody case that has come up in the podcast, but document examined in [Episode 95](e95-mar29) also relates to a family dispute

- Close-Reading: the word 'pubilla' signals that Bernardona is quite young, probably younger than ten years old; 

## What is this document doing?
- This document involves the monarch in a family custody case. 
- The document presumes the power of the state to alter the custody arrangements of a young orphan.

## Questions
- What circumstances would lead a family dispute or civil case to rise to the attention of the monarch? 
- How did Joan find out about Bernardona's plight?
- Was Francesc Vines the uncle of Bernardona? If not, what was their familial relationship? How did Garcia de Loris fit in to this picture?
- Did Francesc Vines and Garcia de Loris promptly comply with Joan's order? 
- Why was the Governor of Valencia involved instead of a lower level bailiff or veguer?
