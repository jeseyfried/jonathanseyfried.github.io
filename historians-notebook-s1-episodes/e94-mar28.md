## Today’s Document 
- Subject: tranfer of properties of Boria and Magallon to Bernat Vilella
- Date: March 28, 1387
- Day of the Week: Thursday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1819 f94r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/4801968)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipients: Mossen Francesc

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: it appears that Bernat Vilella has been a loyal servant and is getting a reward for that in the form of properties formerly owned by Bernat de Fortià; by placing someone loyal in these lands, Violant could gain assurance that her will would carry more authority in locations loyal to her adversaries

- Contextualization: new monarchs often made moves to eject those who had assisted political adversaries in the years prior to the change of rule; consolidation of power in disparate realms would present a difficult challenge for most new kings and queens

- Corroboration: the locations in this document correspond to the ones mentioned in the castigatory letters that Joan and Violant sent to the Governor of Aragon earlier this month, which we examined in [Episode 76](e76-mar10) and [Episode 77](e77-mar11) 

- Close-Reading: the 'scrivanies de justicia' are not familiar to me as a type of office-holder and I think they are like court clerks

## What is this document doing?
- This document secures the ownership of property for a loyal official.
- The document strengthens the ties between a subordinate and the queen by establishing a reward for loyal service.

## Questions
- What was Bernat Vilella's role in connection with the household of Jaume, the three year-old dauphin? 
- Are the properties in Boria and Magallon actually the same ones confiscated from Bernat de Fortià?
- If so, does that mean that the Governor of Aragon had responded to Joan's and Violant's grumpy missives with compliance?
- Who were the 'scrivanies de justicia' and how did their authority relate to that of notaries and other officials?
