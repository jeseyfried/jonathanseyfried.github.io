## Today’s Document 
- Subject: governance of Perpignan Castle
- Date: February 1, 1387
- Day of the Week: Friday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1912 f96v
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12776543)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Joan
- Recipients: Johan del Moli

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: As king, Joan held supreme authority over the governance of royal properties and I think the Perpignan Castle is likely one of those properties

- Contextualization: Claire Ponsich wrote a detailed article about Joan and Violant's customs and furnishings at Perpignan Castle, mostly citing documents from later in their reign[^cp]; it's clear that they filled the space of this castle with splendor 

[^cp]:Claire Ponsich, “Perpignan au féminin, 1380-1431. Quand une femme de pouvoir codifie l’espace, maîtrise l’émotion politique mais libère le sentiment familial.” In *Perpignan une et plurielle*, edited by Raymond Sala and Michelle Ros, 177–203. (Canet: Trabucaire, 2004).

- Corroboration: a document from Martí's register would help with corroborating this; a far deeper knowlege of the history of Perpignan would also inform the meaning behind Joan's decision to support this change of personnel

- Close-Reading: I suspect that, following the examples of some of the other documents, Johan del Moli has performed admirably for Martí and that this plays into whatever development this document traces

## What is this document doing?
- This document reiterates the king's authority over the governance of royal properties in Perpignan.
- The document furthers the cooperation between Joan and Martí in the establishment of the new reign.

## Questions
- Who was Johan del Moli and what was his new role at Perpignan Castle?
- How had Martí been involved in this?
- Why did this document later need the additional squeezed-in note of April 1st?
- Are the rings mentioned in this letter related to the rings sent by Violant to Joan on January 6th?
- What was the larger context for the governance of Perpignan Castle at this time?
- How often had Joan and Violant stayed in Perpignan Castle before their reign?
