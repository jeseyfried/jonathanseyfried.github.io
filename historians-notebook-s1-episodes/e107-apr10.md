## Today’s Document 
- Subject: recommendation for a town vicarage
- Date: April 10, 1387
- Day of the Week: Wednesday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1819 f94v
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/4801968)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipients: Bishop of Lleida

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: as queen, it would be normal for Violant to intervene in ecclesiastical appointments by giving recommendations like this one for a vicar in a small town; what is not so clear is how much Violant herself would have been involved in a lower level posting or if one of her court officials managed this from beginning to end

- Contextualization: the Great Western Schism; social networks as a source of royal power; [Lleida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lleida) as a longtime component of the Crown of Aragon and a cultural center with its university, but [Termens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%C3%A9rmens) was a small town that perhaps had relatively little interaction with that center

- Corroboration: [Episode 46](e46-feb8) and [Episode 48](e48-feb10) also touched on the management of affairs in Lleida; other instances of the monarchs' involvement in ecclesiastical postings were discussed in [Episode 2](e2-dec26) and several other episodes that can be found by using the [tag ecclesiastical](https://jonathanseyfried.net/historians-notebook/tags/?tag=ecclesiastical)

- Close-Reading: line 6 includes the phrase 'servidor del senyor Rey' and this might be referring to Jacme de Beuda, the person being recommended in the letter, indicating a closer connection to the royal couple; this document contains a variation of [Chi-Rho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chi_Rho) to abbreviate for Christ

## What is this document doing?
- This document activates the queen's social network even for a very small posting.
- The document appears to contain the typical formulas for speaking positively about a job candidate.

## Questions
- Who was Jacme de Beuda and what had he done to deserve this recommendation? 
- How much attention did Violant give to this particular appointment? 
- Was the post of Termens vicar important to the Bishop of Lleida? Why or why not?
- Was there anything out of the ordinary about this document in regard to the usual process for recommendations for ecclesiastical posts?
