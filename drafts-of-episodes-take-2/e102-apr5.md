## Today’s Document 
- Subject: documenting the transfer of 500 gold florins to Maria of Sicily
- Date: April 5, 1387
- Day of the Week: Friday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1971 f81r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12778835)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Joan
- Recipient: Maria of Sicily

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: Joan and Maria of Sicily might have been married to each other if Joan's father had gotten his way in 1378; that Maria of Sicily had her own treasurer within the bureaucracy of the Crown of Aragon reveals that she was considered a part of the state

- Contextualization: the Crown of Aragon had ruled Sicily since 1282 but faced intermittant rebellions and resistance; Constance of Aragon, Pere's daughter from his first marriage had married the king of Sicily, Frederick III. Frederick III was a direct descendant of the famous and powerful Frederick II, Holy Roman Emperor who ruled from Sicily, from 1220-1250

- Corroboration: the role of Sicily in the disagreement between Pere the Ceremonious and Joan about Joan's second marriage was also discussed in [Episode 7](e7-dec31); [Episode 40](e40-feb2) included some additional historical context about Sicily

- Close-Reading: the last line of the probata, 'vidit eam dominus Rex,' might indicate that Joan took special actions regarding this transfer of funds

## What is this document doing?
- This document produces the transfer of funds between two financial entities within the state.
- The document refuses to acknowledge any familial relationship beyond the transaction of funds.

## Questions
- Was there anything unusual about this document or was it typical of money transfers within the royal family?
- Where was Maria of Sicily at this time?
- Had Martí the Younger already been betrothed to Maria of Sicily at this time?
- What exactly did the additional information in the probata signify about the production of this document?
