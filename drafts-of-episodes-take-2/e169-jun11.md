## Today’s Document 
- Subject: a messenger from Violant collecting testimony from people in Martí's house
- Date: June 11, 1387
- Day of the Week: Tuesday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R2053 f13r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12778718)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipient: Martí, Joan's brother and the Duke of Montblanch

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: this is the first letter in a long while that I have seen addressed to Martí and this makes me think that Martí was in Barcelona for most of the time between February and June of 1387 

- Contextualization: each of the monarchs had their own household and officers, which functioned in many ways like the royal court; for an idea of what Martí's household would have looked like, it could be helpful to look at scholarship about the households of other dukes and duchesses[^lrd]

[^lrd]:Lledo Ruiz Domingo has done excellent work on the details of Violant's household. Lledó Ruiz Domingo, “Surrounding the Future Queen of the Crown of Aragon: Violant of Bar’s Household as Duchess of Girona (1384–1386),” *Royal Studies Journal* 10, no. 1 (2023): 96-135.

- Corroboration: the document examined in [Episode 60](e60-feb22) also requires obtaining the testimony of someone, but in that case it was a direct summons

- Close-Reading: Violant wrote that there is 'peril in delay' so the matter at hand must have some level of urgency to it, perhaps a serious crime has been committed

## What is this document doing?
- This document presumes that Martí will agree to let these two members of his household sit for an interview with the scribe Violant is sending.
- The document blends family connection with a legal process.

## Questions
- What was the case at hand? Was it a criminal investigaton?
- Where was Martí at this time?
- How would Martí have reacted to this request from Violant? 
- What was the background of the two men identified in this letter?
