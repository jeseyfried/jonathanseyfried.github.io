## Today’s Document 
- Subject: replacing an assessor who was too slow
- Date: June 8, 1387
- Day of the Week: Saturday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1819 f100v-101r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/4801968)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipients: the Governor of Mallorca, lawyer Johan Martin, Petro Tries a magistrate in Mallorca

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: Violant issues the directives in these documents but states that Joan made the decision to fire the previous assessor, Jacme de Vila; it is the likely impetus for this document was some merchants in Barcelona complaining about the delay in the ruling from the current assessor

- Contextualization: Mallorca was a vibrant trade port; several officials worked to assess the value of cargo for taxation purposes and in some ports there were overlapping multiple authorities doing this kind of work

- Corroboration: the document examined in [Episode 69](e69-mar3) corroborates this one in the importance of Mallorca as a center of maritime trade; 

- Close-Reading: the inclusion of the royal procurator in these documents implies a close communication between him and the parties involved in whatever case has come before the assessor; I would like to know the function of the 'estranyera' in this document, whether it is referring to an actual foreigner or if it is a threat of what will happen if there is noncompliance

## What is this document doing?
- This document reinforces the close connections between royal officials and high status merchants.
- The document smooths the way for trade goods to be processed through royal bureaucracy.

## Questions
- Did Violant have the authority to remove the assessor if she had wanted to earlier?
- What was the cargo, or other property, being assessed in the case that caused this problem?
- Why had Jacme de Vila delayed his ruling on the case?
- Had Johan Martin already known about this and was he prepared to take over as assessor?
- Would the Governor and magistrate of Mallorca been resentful about this royal intervention?
- To what extent did Violant consult with Joan about this situation before composing these letters?

## Additional Note
This document helped me to identify the word that starts with 'geren...' before Gubernatoris as a form of gero, gerrere. The meaning amounts to 'the one who currently conducts the office of' Governor.
