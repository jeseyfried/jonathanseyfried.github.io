## Notable Documents from July to December 1387
- July 1, ACA CR R1972 f146r: Joan instructs his procurator in Mallorca to purchase another mappa mundi from the Creseques workshop
- July 2, ACA CR R1923 f992: Bad news from Pisa leads Joan to write to the prohomens of several cities telling them to advise Catalan merchants to avoid Pisa
- July 6, ACA CR R2056 f99v-100r: Violant, in a letter to Maria of Sicily, claims that the sum offered for Yolande of Aragon's dowry is the largest ever dowry offered by a King of the Crown of Aragon
- July 12, ACA CR2053 f16r: Violant writes to Charles III of Navarre that Joan is convalescing well, and does not specificy whether this is from yet another illness or still the one from late April
- July 17, ACA CR R1954 f4v: Joan writes to the Pope about reports of miracles attributed to the recently deceased Cardinal of Luxembourg; Joan also asks the Pope to send him one of the dead Cardinal's rings
- July 25, ACA CR R2053 f19r: Violant writes to the Marquis de Villena about Joan's good health within the context of miracles connected to the recently deceased Cardinal of Luxembourg
- August 17, ACA CR R1751 f96r-97r: Within a long list of items in a letter to the Pope, Joan requests that Violant get the Pope's official release from her pledge to stop wearing jewelry in an effort to gain favor with God in the moments of Joan's illness earlier in the year; no documentary evidence has yet been found in Violant's registers regarding this pledge, which she must have made at some point between January and June of 1387
- August 25, ACA CR R1751 f107r: For reasons having to do with ill health, Joan asks the Pope about a renowned German astrologer
- September 11, ACA CR R2053 30r: Violant tells Luchino Scarampi to return to Avignon to resume the negotiations for the marriage of Yolande of Aragon
- September 15, ACA CR R1760 12r: Joan challenges the authority of the Papal Inquisition in the Crown of Aragon
- September 18, ACA CR Cartas Reales, Juan I, Caja 1, nº117: Joan inquires about the criminal prosecution of Antico Dezpí, who allegedly falsified documents
- September 19, ACA CR R1922 f143r-v: Joan expresses his concern about the overhunting of partridges
- October 8, ACA CR R1952 f48r: Joan thanks Violant's father, Robert de Bar, for several books that he sent
- October 18, ACA CR R2053 f34r: Violant, during her journey to the monastery at Montserrat to give thanks to the Virgin for saving Joan, writes to the king's physician to send her news of Joan's present condition
