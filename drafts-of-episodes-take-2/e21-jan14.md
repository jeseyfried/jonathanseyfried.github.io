## Today’s Document 
- Subject: Joan approves a sale in Girona
- Date: January 14, 1387
- Day of the Week: Monday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1952 f9v
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/12777462)
- Place: Granollers
- Sender: Joan
- Recipients: Prohomens of Girona

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: in anticipation of all the new business coming his way as king, Joan might be looking to tie up all the loose ends from his duties as Duke of Girona; 

- Contextualization: the Kings of the Crown of Aragon did routinely intervene about very granular matters; I have some open questions about the expectations for what level of issues would routinely rise to the king's attention - perhaps I have a too-modern assumption about this

- Corroboration: it is difficult for me to understand the division of labor in diplomacy and governance when I notice that Violant writes to the Pope at about the same time that Joan writes up an approval paper for what seems like a minor property transaction in a second tier city; I have seen Ramon de Soler in several of Joan's documents and I think he was the main money man for Joan at this time

- Close-Reading: in the second line, is it all property categories or something else - patios or pigs; 'sperend' appears at the end of this document and the other one on this folio and I'm not sure what that means - possibly Esperandeu Cardona

## What is this document doing?
- This document demonstrates the willingness of high officials to intervene in minor transactions. (Unless I am mis-interpreting the document, which could very well be the case here.)

## Questions
- Is this document all about properties or are those 'porches' actually boars?
- What level of urgency was this item of business for Joan? Why is he occupied by this during an intensely busy time?
- Was this a substantial sale? Did it affect Joan's revenues in any way?
- What possible disputes might the seller, Bernguer, have encountered in this situation?
