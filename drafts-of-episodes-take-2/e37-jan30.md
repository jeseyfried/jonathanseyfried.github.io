## Today’s Document 
- Subject: A land sale in Maresme
- Date: January 30, 1387
- Day of the Week: Wednesday
- Language: Latin
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1819 f79r
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/4801968)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipients: officials of Maresme

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: Violant recently inherited a very large number of land holdings of various types and now has the opportunity to make money off of them; the leaders in the Maresme province might have requested a change in the ownership of the royal holdings in their region

- Contextualization: Joan and Violant incurred tremendous criticism for selling off royal land, both from their contemporaries and later historians[^djk]; this document also fits in with the larger pattern of Iberian queens administering a portfolio of their own revenues based on land ownership

[^djk]:Donald J. Kagay, “Poetry in the Dock: The Court Culture of Joan I on Trial (1396-1398),” in Donald J. Kagay, *War, Government, and Society in the Medieval Crown of Aragon*, Variorum Collected Studies Series, CS 861 (Aldershot, UK: Ashgate/Variorum, 2007), XI, 48–99

- Corroboration: the document examined in [Episode 6](historians-notebook-s1-episodes/e6-dec30) corroborates this one, in that Violant now holds authority over the portfolio of the Queen of the Crown of Aragon; other documents that mention Alatzario Golluff will provide good corroboration for how this financial transaction relates to others during Violant's reign

- Close-Reading: the word 'apocha' is used to indicate a receipt of a payment and apocham appears in this document at the end of line 4; this document definitely records information about a financial transaction but it is not certain to me at this point that Violant's account is receiving the money

## What is this document doing?
- This document invokes state power to enact and codify a transfer of land ownership in Maresme.
- The document also demonstrates Violant's position as a financial manager.

## Questions
- Did promiment people in Maresme request this sale?
- What was Alatzario Golluff's station and position within the treasury?
- Is Violant gaining money from the sale described in this document?
- Were the lands in Maresme part of Sibilla de Fortia's portfolio before January 1387?
- How much money is being exchanged in the transaction described in this document?
- Would this be seen as a sensible financial decision or was this a sale that provided short term gain at a long-term loss?
