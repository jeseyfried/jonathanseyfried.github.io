## Recommended Background Activity
Students will benefit from a prior activity that helps them to understand how historians create narratives from at times very disparate sources. An excellent lesson available on the [Reacting Consortium website](https://reactingconsortium.org/games/makinghistory) presents students with isolated snippets regarding a relationship that ended in a breakup. The content can be adapted for younger students.
