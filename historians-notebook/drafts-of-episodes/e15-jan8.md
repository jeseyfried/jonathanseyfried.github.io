## Today’s Document 
- Subject: Violant establishes contact with Crown of Aragon administrators in Majorca
- Date: January 8, 1387
- Day of the Week: Tuesday
- Language: Catalan
- Archival Reference Number: ACA CR R1819 f72v
- [Link to PARES](https://pares.mcu.es/ParesBusquedas20/catalogo/show/4801968)
- Place: Barcelona
- Sender: Violant
- Recipient: Governor of Majorca 

## Historical Thinking Notes
- Sourcing: this appears to be another example of Violant surveying all of the important territories and establishing authority over them

- Contextualization: throughout the Middle Ages, Majorca was a major hub in Mediterranean trade and travel; at times, Majorca resisted Aragonese rule, but mostly stayed within the Crown of Aragon during the Late Middle Ages

- Corroboration: this parallels the outreach to the Count of Empúries 

- Close-Reading: as a short missive, this letter seems to exhibit a rather stripped-down administrative tone

## What is this document doing?
- This document furthers the project of creating new ties between territorial administrators and the new monarchs
- The document reveals more about the extent of the territorial holdings of the late fourteenth-century Crown of Aragon

## Questions
- What was the geo-political status of Majorca at this time?
- Violant says that Joan asked her to write to the Governor of Majorca - does this mean that Majorca is in Violant's portfolio? Or is it a temporary measure while Joan is ill?
