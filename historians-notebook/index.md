# The Historian's Notebook

Season 1, [*Molt Cara Companyona*](/historians-notebook-s1/), examines the first six months of the reign of Joan I of Aragon and Violant de Bar.

Season 2, [*The Maximum of Information*](/historians-notebook-s2/), coming in November 2026, looks at the community of ufologists in the second half of the twentieth century.

Season 3, not yet titled, will be released in 2027 and will focus on a single 1890s scrapbook all about domestic cats.
